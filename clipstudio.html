Keep this page open and the screen on while exporting. Exporting runs at normal playing speed, so a 30 second clip takes about 30 seconds.

How it works: Clip Studio listens to the sound and watches the picture while your video plays quickly, finds scene changes, then ranks every second by loudness and movement and picks the strongest windows, starting and ending at natural points. It does not understand speech or meaning, so check each clip and adjust the start and end if needed. Clips are saved as MP4 when your phone supports it, otherwise WebM.
